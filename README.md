# Campus-Cravings - Food delivery for students by students 
# Inspiration

Campus life is busy, and many students struggle to balance part-time jobs with classes. We wanted to create a flexible way for students to earn money while helping others get food delivered conveniently—without disrupting their studies.

# What it does

Campus-Cravings is a mobile app designed for campus students:

1. Earn while delivering:** Students can earn money by delivering food to fellow students while getting their own.
2. Convenient ordering:** Students who need food can place an order from anywhere on campus and have it delivered straight to their seat.
3. Campus-exclusive:** The system is limited to campus, making it fast and reliable.

Essentially, it provides a second way of earning for students who don’t want a traditional part-time job and offers convenience for those receiving food.

# How we built it

* Front-end:** Mobile interface with an intuitive design for ordering and tracking food.
* Back-end:** Handled student accounts, orders, and payments.
* APIs:** Used the Leaflet API for mapping delivery locations on campus.
* Payment & rewards:** Implemented a system to manage payments and earnings for student deliverers.

We focused on a smooth user experience, ensuring that both ordering and delivering food is seamless.

# Challenges we ran into

* Mapping & location tracking:** Integrating campus-wide locations accurately required learning and adapting the Leaflet API.
* Concurrent users:** Managing multiple orders and deliveries simultaneously without confusion was tricky.
* Balancing earning and delivery:** Ensuring that students delivering food could get their own order quickly while completing deliveries efficiently.

# Accomplishments that we are proud of

* Successfully implemented campus-wide delivery mapping using the Leaflet API.
* Built a system where students can earn while delivering their own orders, creating a unique earning model.
* Developed a fully functional app in a short timeframe, ensuring a smooth and intuitive user experience.

# What we learned

* Practical experience with location-based services and API integration.
* Designing an app that balances user convenience with earning efficiency.
* Coordinating effectively in a team under hackathon time constraints.
